The Story Behind the Journey

About Thais

Welcome to my space! I believe that when you truly want something and set your mind to it, anything is possible.

Rota da Vida was born from the desire to share my real experiences with the world. I’m 34 years old, Brazilian and passionate about travel and discovering new ways of living. I’ve been traveling solo since I was 22, and in 2021, I moved to Portugal, where I lived for several years. I have a Bachelor’s degree in Physical Education, although I chose not to pursue a career in the field. I’m now transitioning into the world of technology and data science, exploring new skills and building a new professional path. Recently, I took a six-month sabbatical to travel around Asia, an experience that gave me the opportunity to step outside my comfort zone, learn more about myself, and see the world from a different perspective. Here, I share a little about my journey, my love for travel, and the lessons I’ve learned along the way.
