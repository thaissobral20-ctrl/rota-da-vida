🇲🇹 Malta: A 7-Day Mediterranean Itinerary

7 days of paradise, history, beautiful beaches, and Mediterranean charm

By Thais • September 2023 • Mediterranean

I visited Malta in September 2023 and spent seven days exploring this incredible Mediterranean country. Malta is a delicious mix of Mediterranean culture, Italian-inspired food, beautiful beaches, historic cities, and relatively affordable prices for anyone looking to enjoy the European summer without breaking the bank.

✈️ Flight from Lisbon

Direct flight with Air Malta for ~€140 (punctual & easy).

🏡 Where to Stay

St. Julian's • €200 for 7 nights in a hostel with pool & views.

🚌 Getting Around

7-day unlimited public transit pass for ~€20.

🏡 Where to Stay: St. Julian's

I stayed in St. Julian's, and I highly recommend the area. It is close to bars, restaurants, beaches, and public transportation, making it a convenient base for exploring the island.

I stayed at a wonderful hostel with a swimming pool and beautiful views, which was the perfect combination of comfort and affordability. Accommodation: €200 for 7 nights.

✈️ Getting to Malta

I found a direct flight from Lisbon to Malta with Air Malta for around €140. The flight was easy and punctual, and I thought it was a great option for getting to Malta from Portugal.

🚌 Getting Around

Public transportation was one of the easiest and most affordable ways to explore Malta. I bought a 7-day unlimited public transport pass for around €20, which was definitely worth it because I used the buses quite a lot throughout the trip.

🗺️ 7-Day Malta Itinerary

Day 1 — Valletta 🇲🇹

I started my trip by taking the bus to Valletta, Malta's historic capital.

I spent the day walking through the historic center, exploring its beautiful streets and architecture.

Of course, I also had to try the local food! I stopped for a delicious pasta dish that cost only €8.

Malta's cuisine has strong Italian influences, so if you're a pasta lover, you're definitely in the right place! 🍝

Highlights:
  • Valletta Historic Centre
  • Historic streets and buildings
  • Local Maltese cuisine
  • Baroque architecture

Day 2 — Golden Bay & St. Peter's Pool 🏖️

It was time to enjoy Malta's incredible coastline!

I visited Golden Bay, one of the island's beautiful beaches, and then headed to St. Peter's Pool.

St. Peter's Pool is especially unique because of its natural rock formations, which create little natural pools that look almost like jacuzzis.

The scenery was absolutely surreal!

Highlights:
  • Golden Bay
  • St. Peter's Pool
  • Swimming
  • Natural rock formations

Day 3 — Gozo Island 🇲🇹

One of the highlights of my trip was taking the ferry to Gozo Island.

The journey took around one hour, and once we arrived, we headed to Mġarr ix-Xini, a stunning beach surrounded by dramatic scenery.

It was one of the most beautiful places I visited during the trip.

Later in the day, we went up to the Cittadella, the historic fortified city of Gozo.

And then came the perfect ending: an unforgettable sunset overlooking the island. 🌅

Highlights:
  • Ferry to Gozo
  • Mġarr ix-Xini
  • Cittadella
  • Sunset in Gozo

Day 4 — Blue Lagoon & Comino 🩵

No trip to Malta would be complete without visiting the famous Blue Lagoon on Comino Island.

We took a boat trip to the island and spent the day enjoying the incredibly clear turquoise water.

The color of the sea was simply out of this world!

And, of course, there was gelato. 🍦 I had one of the best gelatos of my life — lemon with meringue, or something very similar, haha!

Highlights:
  • Boat trip
  • Comino Island
  • Blue Lagoon
  • Swimming
  • Gelato!

Day 5 — Sliema 🌊

We spent the day exploring Sliema and enjoying some of the beaches and coastal areas nearby.

We also visited a restaurant from the same franchise featured in the movie Eat Pray Love, which added a fun little pop-culture experience to the day.

Sliema was a great place to slow down, enjoy the sea, and simply take in the Mediterranean atmosphere.

Highlights:
  • Sliema
  • Coastal walks
  • Beaches
  • Mediterranean restaurants

Day 6 — A Free Day to Explore 🕊️

After several days of sightseeing, I would recommend keeping one day flexible.

Use it to revisit a place you loved, relax by the sea, explore some smaller beaches, or simply wander around without a strict schedule.

One of my favorite things about traveling is leaving a little room for spontaneity — sometimes the best moments aren't on the itinerary.

Day 7 — Mdina 🏰

On my last day, I explored Mdina, this time on my own.

Known as the Silent City, Mdina is a historic fortified city filled with narrow streets, beautiful architecture, and a unique medieval atmosphere. It was the perfect way to finish my trip.

If you love history, architecture, and medieval cities, Mdina is definitely a must-visit in Malta.

Highlights:
  • Mdina Old Town
  • Medieval streets
  • Historic architecture
  • City walls
  • Exploring at your own pace

💰 My Malta Travel Budget

Expense Cost
✈️ Return flight from Lisbon €140
🏡 7 nights in St. Julian's €200

🍝 Food & activities Variable

Why I loved Malta 💛

Malta surprised me with how much it offers in such a small country. In just seven days, I experienced beautiful beaches, historic cities, Mediterranean food, islands, boat trips, and incredible sunsets.

And although I traveled solo, I wasn't really alone. I met two amazing Portuguese girls at my hostel, and we ended up spending almost the entire trip together. It was one of those unexpected connections that makes traveling even more special.

Malta was a reminder that traveling alone doesn't necessarily mean being alone — sometimes it simply means being open to the people you meet along the way.
