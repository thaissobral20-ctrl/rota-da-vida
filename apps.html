Travel Tech & Budget Toolkit

📱 Essential Travel Apps: Explore Smarter & Save Money

The exact applications I rely on for flights, accommodation, navigation, and budget management on every journey.

By Thais • Practical Travel Tools • Updated 2026

Traveling light and with peace of mind doesn't require dozens of tools. A curated set of reliable smartphone apps can save you hundreds of euros, keep you from getting lost, and make solo travel safe and effortless.

✈️ Booking & Flights

Skyscanner & Google Flights Flights & Alerts

How I use it: Never buy a ticket before cross-checking dates on calendar view. Setting price alerts months ahead is how I secured my direct flight from Lisbon to Malta for €140.

💡 Pro Tip: Always clear your browser cookies or search in incognito mode before booking the final fare directly on the airline's official website.
Open Skyscanner ↗ Open Google Flights ↗
Hostelworld & Booking.com Accommodation

How I use it: For solo trips, Hostelworld is king for reading recent guest reviews and finding properties with social common areas and swimming pools (like St. Julian's in Malta). Booking.com is my go-to for flexible cancellation options.

Open Hostelworld ↗ Open Booking.com ↗

🚌 Getting Around & Navigation

FlixBus & Omio Ground Transportation

How I use it: The backbone of my Central Europe trip! FlixBus allowed me to jump between Budapest, Bratislava, Vienna, and Prague for €5 to €8 per segment. Omio compares trains versus regional buses across Europe instantly.

Open FlixBus ↗ Open Omio ↗
Citymapper & Google Maps Urban Navigation

How I use it: Citymapper gives real-time departure counts for metro, trams, and buses in large cities like Vienna and Budapest. Download offline maps on Google Maps beforehand so you never get stranded without mobile signal.

Open Citymapper ↗ Open Google Maps ↗

💶 Money & Currency

Wise & Revolut Zero Hidden Fee Cards

How I use it: Indispensable when traveling through countries with different currencies (like Hungarian Forint in Budapest or Czech Koruna in Prague). You convert funds at the interbank rate and avoid predatory airport exchange kiosks.

Open Wise ↗ Open Revolut ↗
Splitwise & TravelSpend Expense Tracking

How I use it: When meeting travel buddies (like the girls I met in Malta), Splitwise tracks shared dinners, groceries, and boat rentals seamlessly without awkward money talks at the end of the trip. TravelSpend tracks daily budgets per category.

Open Splitwise ↗ Open TravelSpend ↗
📲
Essential Pre-Trip Checklist:

Download your transit tickets to your phone wallet, take screenshots of hotel confirmation addresses, and keep offline translations ready on Google Translate before arriving!
