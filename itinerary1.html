Itinerary & Backpacking Guide

❄️ Mini Winter Backpacking Trip Through Central Europe

Budapest → Bratislava → Vienna → Prague

By Thais • Winter Journey • Central Europe

A winter backpacking adventure through four beautiful European cities, traveling mostly by bus and exploring each destination on foot and by public transportation.

🇭🇺 Budapest

2 Days • Baths, Ruin Bars & Kürtőskalács

🇸🇰 Bratislava

1 Day • Devín Castle via €5 FlixBus

🇦🇹 Vienna

2 Days • Snowy Palaces & Austrian Opera

🇨🇿 Prague

2 Days • Historic Magic & Sunset at Botel Vodník

🇭🇺 Budapest, Hungary — 2 Days

I started my adventure in Budapest, a beautiful city full of history and charm. The city is divided into two parts, Buda and Pest, separated by the famous Danube River.

Budapest has a large city center, and because I visited during winter, I relied quite a lot on public transportation to escape the cold. I bought a 72-hour public transport pass, which was very convenient.

💡
Important Transit Tip:

Always validate your ticket before using public transportation. Otherwise, you can get fined — as I learned the hard way in Prague! 😅

📍 Things to do

I explored a lot of Budapest on foot and visited some of the city's main attractions:

  • Hungarian Parliament Building
  • Buda Castle
  • Great Market Hall
  • Margaret Island
  • Városliget (City Park)
  • Outdoor thermal baths
  • Ice skating
  • Szimpla Kert

I also tried some traditional Hungarian food. I had kürtőskalács, a delicious chimney cake with chocolate inside, and tasted the traditional goulash.

At night, I visited Szimpla Kert, one of Budapest's most famous ruin bars. The atmosphere was completely different from anything I had experienced before!

I spent two days in Budapest, but honestly, I would have loved to stay for one more.

🇸🇰 Bratislava, Slovakia — 1 Day

From Budapest, I took a FlixBus to Bratislava. The ticket cost around €5, and the journey took approximately 2.5 hours.

I only spent the day in Bratislava and didn't stay overnight. For me, that was enough to explore the main sights.

I walked around the historic city center and then headed to Devín Castle, a beautiful historic site with stunning views of the surrounding area.

By the end of the afternoon, I was already on my way to Austria.

Travel tip: Bratislava works really well as a day trip when traveling between Budapest and Vienna.

🇦🇹 Vienna, Austria — 2 Days

From Bratislava, I took another FlixBus to Vienna, with a journey of around one hour.

I spent two days exploring Vienna and, despite the intense cold and snow, managed to see quite a lot of the city. The snow actually made everything feel even more magical! ❄️

📍 Things to do

  • Schönbrunn Palace
  • St. Stephen's Cathedral
  • Historic city center
  • Traditional Austrian opera

One of the highlights of my time in Vienna was attending a traditional opera. It was one of the most memorable experiences of the entire trip, and I would definitely recommend it — even if classical music isn't usually your thing.

You can explore many parts of Vienna on foot, but because the temperatures were extremely low, I ended up using public transportation quite often.

🇨🇿 Prague, Czech Republic — 2 Days

From Vienna, I continued to Prague. The journey took around four hours, and I paid approximately €8.

Of all the destinations on this backpacking trip, Prague felt like the most affordable, at least from my perspective.

I stayed at a really cool hostel with a bar in the basement and live music, which made the experience even more fun.

📍 Things to do

Prague is simply charming! The Old Town Square is one of the city's highlights and is home to the famous Astronomical Clock, which attracts crowds throughout the day.

If you love architecture and beautiful historic buildings, Prague is a dream. I visited:

  • Old Town Square
  • Astronomical Clock
  • Dancing House
  • St. Nicholas Church
  • John Lennon Wall
  • Charles Bridge
  • Prague Castle
  • Botel Vodník

One of my favorite moments was watching the sunset from Botel Vodník. It was one of those simple travel moments that stays with you forever. ✨

⚠️
Important transportation tip (The €45 Lesson):

Even though I had purchased a 3-day public transport ticket, I received a €45 fine because I didn't know I had to validate it before using it. So, once again: Always validate your public transport ticket! 😅

🎒 My Central Europe Route

Destination Time Transportation
🇭🇺 Budapest 2 days —
🇸🇰 Bratislava 1 day FlixBus (~€5)
🇦🇹 Vienna 2 days FlixBus (~€5)
🇨🇿 Prague 2 days Bus (~€8)

✨ Total: 7 Days of Winter Memories

This was a short but unforgettable winter backpacking adventure through Central Europe. Traveling between these four cities by bus made the route relatively easy to organize, and each destination had a completely different atmosphere.

If I were to do it again, I would probably add a few extra days to Budapest and Vienna — especially because traveling in winter means that the cold can slow you down a little!
